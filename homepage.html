Bin-GO!

A webcam-based anti-litter reward app that watches a user dispose of trash and grants points when the system detects a valid throw/disposal action.

We don’t just track recycling; we reward it.

Who are we?

We are Neel, Mitt and Hector, three students at RCHK who are all invested in technology and have decent knowledge in coding. We have never participated in a ESF COCO hackathon, however we hope to win!
